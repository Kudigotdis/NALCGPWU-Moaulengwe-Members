Scheme Details & Requirements
Everything you need to know to apply for the educational bursary.

What is it & What does it cover?
What is it?
The BOPEU Educational Bursary is a dedicated financial assistance program provided exclusively to union members to help them pursue both short and long-term educational courses.
Approved Uses:
•  Tuition Fees
•  Accommodation related to studies
•  Educational Books & Materials
•  Academic Research Costs

Who Qualifies? (Accordion)
To be eligible for the Educational Bursary, applicants must meet the following criteria:
• Must be a fully registered and active member of BOPEU.
• Must have been consistently subscribing to the union for a minimum period of 6 months prior to application.
• Must demonstrate ability to repay the advance within the stipulated 24-month period.

Required Documents & How to Apply (accordion)
Request application form from info@bopeu.org.bw
• Certified copy of Omang (National ID)
• Most recent current Payslip
• Official Admission Letter from the Institution
• Valid Quotation/Invoice from the Institution

(Accordion) How to Apply: Request application form from info@bopeu.org.bw, complete it in full, attach all required documents, and submit it to your nearest BOPEU regional office.

WHO ARE WE? (Accordion)
Botswana Public Employees Union (BOPEU) is a trade union duly registered in terms of the Trade Unions and Employers' Organization Act and fully recognized by the employer as a bargaining agent for its members. BOPEU was founded in 2007 through the unionization transformation of the then-Botswana Civil Servants Association (BCSA).
BCSA evolved from the merge of the Bechuanaland Protectorate Junior Civil Service Association and the Bechuanaland Protectorate Senior European Service Association in 1960. The history of these two associations dates back to 1942.
BOPEU is a formidable force committed to safeguarding the rights and welfare of public employees in Botswana both from government and parastatals. With a history of advocacy and activism, BOPEU remains dedicated to promoting justice, transparency, and fairness in the workplace.

WHAT WE STAND FOR? (Accordion)
• Effective labour representation
• Protecting and representing workers on labour matters
• Fair wages, safe workplaces, and improved working conditions
• Social justice, equality, and good governance
• Empowered, informed, and united public employees
• Solidarity with workers locally, regionally, and internationally

WHAT WE DO FOR OUR MEMBERS? (Accordion)
- Legal Representation
We stand up for members and speak on your behalf at the workplace, sectoral, and national levels, up to the Apex Court on labour matters as guided by the Constitution.
- Organising Members
We recruit, organise, and mobilise public employees to strengthen unity, solidarity, and collective power within the Union.
- Dispute resolution
We stand up for our members by representing and supporting them in the resolution of workplace disputes, ensuring that their rights, dignity, and conditions of service are protected.
- Collective Bargaining
We negotiate with employers and the government for better salaries, benefits, and fair terms and conditions of service for members.
- Grievance Management
We support members in raising and resolving workplace conflicts in a fair, lawful, and respectful manner.
- Disciplinary Hearings
We assist members in responding to Show Cause Letters and represent them in disciplinary proceedings to ensure that all processes are conducted with procedural and substantive fairness.

HOW DO I BECOME A MEMBER?
• Fill in membership form, KYC form and Group Life Assurance(GLA) form.
• Attach certified copy of ID (for citizens) or certified copy of passport, recent payslip and recent confirmation of employment letter.
• Submit at your workplace through a shop steward or visit any BOPEU office.

WHO CAN JOIN?
All eligible public sector employees across ministries, departments, parastatals, and local authorities. Membership is open to both fixed and non-fixed contract employees.
