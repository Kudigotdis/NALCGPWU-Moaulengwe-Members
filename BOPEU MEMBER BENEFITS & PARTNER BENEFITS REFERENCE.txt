BOPEU
MEMBER BENEFITS & PARTNER BENEFITS REFERENCE
2026 MASTER CONTENT DOCUMENT
Purpose: consolidate BOPEU member benefits and the currently documented benefits or service offerings associated with the 16 named partner businesses. This version is designed for editing by BOPEU and for later conversion into app-ready member benefit cards.



Prepared for BOPEU stakeholder review and digital member-experience planning

1. Executive Summary
BOPEU currently presents membership as extending beyond workplace representation into education, lifestyle savings, utilities, finance, insurance, health and wellness. The official BOPEU site describes selected retailer and hotel discounts, airtime and building-material schemes, access to Babereki SACCOS, funeral cover and financial assistance. The supplied BOPEU site extract also records more than 37,000 members and five offices.
The 16 partners requested for this document span banking, insurance, telecommunications, hospitality, healthcare, dental care, construction materials and borehole services. The strongest publicly documented partner-specific benefits located for this working version are Archein Hardware, Diagnofirm, Access Bank and First Capital Bank/Babereki. Old Mutual, Hollard and the historic Mascom/Orange airtime arrangements are also documented, but some current prices, discounts or eligibility terms are not publicly stated in the material reviewed.
Where a specific discount, rate, repayment term or service entitlement could not be verified, this document deliberately says so. This protects BOPEU from publishing a benefit promise that may differ from the active partner agreement. The final app should display only benefits that BOPEU has approved and confirmed with each partner.
Overall rule for publication: use exact, current partner terms; include expiry dates where applicable; require the BOPEU membership card where the partner requires verification; and show “Terms & Conditions apply” for credit, finance and insurance products.
2. Verification Status Used in This Document
Status	Meaning

PARTNER SERVICE / TERMS NOT PUBLIC	The partner and its service scope are documented or supplied, but the current BOPEU member discount/rate is not publicly stated in the sources reviewed.
HISTORICAL / CURRENT TERMS TO VERIFY	A BOPEU-linked benefit is documented in an older source, but the available evidence is not sufficient to present the old terms as the current 2026 offer.

21. Recommended App Content Model
Use the same content fields for every partner so members can scan benefits consistently. The app should not mix a verified discount with an unverified service description.
Field	Purpose
Partner name	Display name shown on the benefit card.
Category	Banking, Insurance, Health, Hospitality, Telecom, Hardware, Water, Dental, etc.
Headline benefit	One sentence, e.g. “Up to P200,000 building-material credit”.
Benefit type	Discount, Credit, Loan, Special rate, Service access, Insurance, Airtime scheme, Wellness benefit, etc.
Value / amount	Numeric value only when verified.
Eligibility	Active BOPEU member, good standing, membership duration, salary criteria, medical-aid status, etc.
How to access	Branch, WhatsApp, phone, web booking, partner outlet or BOPEU office.
Verification	Membership card, membership number, BOPEU referral, code, payslip or other document.
Validity	Effective date, expiry date or “ongoing until changed”.
Terms & Conditions	Short summary plus link to full terms where available.
Last verified	Date BOPEU last confirmed the offer.
Status	Active / Temporary / Expired / Under review.

24. Governance Checklist Before Going Live
    • Obtain written confirmation from each partner of the current BOPEU member benefit, percentage/rate, eligible services/products, exclusions and expiry date.
    • Maintain a “Last Verified” date and named BOPEU owner for each benefit.
    • Do not convert “special rates” into a percentage discount unless the partner has supplied the percentage or price list.
    • Do not reuse historical airtime, insurance or partnership terms as current terms without a fresh confirmation.
    • For lending and insurance, display required eligibility, affordability/underwriting language and “Terms & Conditions apply”.
    • For health services, display only factual access/price information and avoid claims about treatment outcomes.
    • Require the BOPEU membership card where the partner uses it for verification.
    • Give members a direct contact route for questions, complaints or disputes relating to a partner benefit.
    • Keep the app content synchronized with a central BOPEU-approved benefit register so that changes can be made once and reflected everywhere.
