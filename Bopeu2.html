What is it & what does it cover? ▼
A dedicated financial assistance programme provided exclusively to union members to pursue short and long-term educational courses.
  • Tuition Fees
  • Accommodation related to studies
  • Educational Books & Materials
  • Academic Research Costs
Who qualifies? ▼
  • Fully registered and active BOPEU member
  • Consistently subscribing for a minimum of 6 months prior to application
  • Must demonstrate ability to repay within 24 months
Required documents ▼
  • Certified copy of Omang (National ID)
  • Most recent current payslip
  • Official Admission Letter from the institution
  • Valid Quotation / Invoice from the institution

FAQ
Who are we? ▼
The Botswana Public Employees Union (BOPEU) is a trade union duly registered in terms of the Trade Unions and Employers' Organization Act and fully recognized by the employer as a bargaining agent for its members.

BOPEU was founded in 2007 through the unionization transformation of the then-Botswana Civil Servants Association (BCSA). BCSA evolved from the merge of the Bechuanaland Protectorate Junior Civil Service Association and the Bechuanaland Protectorate Senior European Service Association in 1960. The history of these two associations dates back to 1942.

BOPEU is a formidable force committed to safeguarding the rights and welfare of public employees in Botswana both from government and parastatals.
What we stand for ▼
  • Effective labour representation
  • Protecting and representing workers on labour matters
  • Fair wages, safe workplaces, and improved working conditions
  • Social justice, equality, and good governance
  • Empowered, informed, and united public employees
  • Solidarity with workers locally, regionally, and internationally
What we do for our members ▼
Legal Representation — we stand up for members at workplace, sectoral and national levels, up to the Apex Court on labour matters.

Organising Members — we recruit, organise and mobilise public employees.

Dispute Resolution — we represent members in resolving workplace disputes.

Collective Bargaining — we negotiate with employers and government for better salaries and fair terms.

Grievance Management — we support members in raising and resolving workplace conflicts.

Disciplinary Hearings — we assist members with Show Cause Letters and represent them in disciplinary proceedings.
How do I become a member? ▼
  • Fill in the membership form, KYC form and Group Life Assurance (GLA) form.
  • Attach certified copy of ID (citizens) or certified copy of passport, recent payslip and recent confirmation of employment letter.
  • Submit at your workplace through a shop steward or visit any BOPEU office.
Who can join? ▼
All eligible public sector employees across ministries, departments, parastatals, and local authorities. Membership is open to both fixed and non-fixed contract employees.
