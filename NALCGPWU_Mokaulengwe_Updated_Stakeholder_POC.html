The Mokaulengwe Benefit Scheme offers comprehensive family protection at affordable rates.

Waiting Periods: Immediate vs Extended Family

Understanding waiting periods is critical to avoiding claim delays during bereavement.

Immediate Family (1 Month Waiting Period)

Coverage for the member and spouse activates upon the first successful payroll deduction. No waiting period for accidental death.

Extended Family (6 Months Waiting Period)

Parents and extended family members require 6 consecutive months of premium deductions before coverage becomes active. This applies to natural causes only.

Important Exceptions

  • Accidental Death: No waiting period - pays immediately
  • Policy Reinstatement: Waiting period restarts if premium lapses
  • New Members: 6-month wait for existing extended family on old policies

Activation Timeline

Month 1: First deduction = immediate family activated

Month 6: 6th consecutive deduction = extended family activated

How to Access the Union Member Loan Scheme

The NALCGPWU loan scheme provides quick access to emergency financing for union members.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Active NALCGPWU member in good standing
  • Minimum 6 months membership
  • Consistent payroll deductions (no lapses)
  • Employed in eligible sector (Central Government, Local Council, Parastatal, Landboard)

Borrowing Limits

Maximum loan is calculated based on monthly salary and sector:

  • Up to 3 months gross salary (varies by sector)
  • Repayable via automatic payroll stop-order
  • Interest rates: 8% - 12% per annum (sector dependent)

Application Process

  1. Use the Loan Eligibility Checker in the Claims tab
  2. Provide employment and salary details
  3. Authorize payroll deduction (stop-order)
  4. Receive approval within 24-48 hours
  5. Funds transferred directly to your bank account

Contact Your Cluster Office

For loan inquiries and personalized assistance, reach out to your nearest cluster office listed in the Menu tab.

Fast-Tracking Claims: Essential Document Checklist

Having all required documents ready will significantly speed up the claims process during a difficult time.

For Main Member & Spouse Claims

  • KYC and claim discharge form
  • Certified death certificate
  • Certified copy of Omang (ID)
  • Proof of residential (Affidavit or Utility Bill)
  • Proof of banking details (Bank confirmation letter)
  • Nomination form (Policy document)
  • Marriage certificate and Form B (for spouse)

For Child Claims

  • All of the above, plus:
  • Certified copy of child's birth certificate showing parent details
  • Pregnancy summary report (for still-born claims)

For Parent/Extended Family Claims

  • All standard documents listed above
  • Certified marriage certificate (if relevant for surname difference)
  • Affidavit for surname difference (if applicable)

Processing Timeline

Submission Deadline: Within 6 months of death

Document Deadline: All documents within 12 months

Payment Timeline: Within 48 hours of approved claim (funds to nominated bank account)

Pro Tips

  • Make certified copies of all documents BEFORE submission
  • Contact your cluster office for certification assistance
  • Keep receipts and submission confirmations
  • Use WhatsApp to send documents if offline - attach screenshots
