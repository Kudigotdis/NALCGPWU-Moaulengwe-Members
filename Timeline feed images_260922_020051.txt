1. Complete Family Protection Insurance
BOPEU Funeral Cover
BOPEU provides a group funeral scheme underwritten by Metropolitan Life, designed to provide financial protection to members and their families when a death occurs. 
Key benefits:
• P60,000 core benefit
• Compulsory cover for the member, spouse and children under 21
• 12-month premium waiver included
• No medical tests
• Easy claims process
• Quick settlement
• Discounted cover specifically available to BOPEU members
• Subscription is 1% of basic salary, with the stated subscription range capped between P39.90 and P100.00. 
Family benefit structure
The published BOPEU information also specifies benefits for children according to age:

2. Emergency Fund Available — Union Fund Scheme
There are two different financial-support facilities in the BOPEU information, so I would not call both of them an “emergency fund” without clarification.
BOPEU Emergency Loan
BOPEU's in-house lending scheme includes an Emergency Loan designed for members who need short-term financial assistance.
Emergency Loan:
• P2,001 – P20,000
• Repayment period of up to 24 months
• Published information states a minimum loan of P1,000
• Approval turnaround stated as 48 hours. 
The wider BOPEU benefits information describes its financial-assistance service as providing emergency loans and financial advisory services to support members' economic stability. 
Babereki / Union financial support
BOPEU also provides access to Babereki SACCOS, described as providing cooperative savings and credit solutions for members. 
Important: The material you supplied does not establish a separate product formally named “Emergency Fund Available Union Fund Scheme.” If that is the exact name used internally by BOPEU, I would have BOPEU confirm the official name, contribution rules, maximum amount and eligibility before putting those details into the app.
Timeline headline:
“Financial Support When Life Takes an Unexpected Turn.”
