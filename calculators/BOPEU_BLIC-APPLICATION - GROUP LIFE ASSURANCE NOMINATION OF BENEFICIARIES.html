The terms and conditions of cover in this policy apply for the duration of the policy. These cannot be modified except in writing by an authorized official of Babereki Life Insurance Company (Pty) Ltd.

The company relied on the information provided by the Policyholder to issue this policy. If all material information has not been disclosed, the company may void the policy from the policy date and retain some or all the premiums received.

By accepting this contract, you give Babereki Life Insurance Company (Pty) Ltd. consent to retain your personal information and to use and share this information with legitimate sources only for the purpose of this insurance contract.

The maximum age of entry is 65 years for the member.

A Life Assured’s Assurance cover shall cease on the earliest of the following:

  1. The Life Assured reaching 60 years for Total & Permanent Disability Cover. There is no cease age for Death Cover.
  2. Termination of this Policy by the Insurer or the Policyholder.
  3. Cessation of payment of premiums.
  4. The Life Assured ceasing to be an Eligible Member.

A waiting period of six (6) months will apply to the member. This is after the starting or reinstatement date of the policy, unless death is due to an accident. The company is not obligated to settle any accidental death claim before it has received the first premium.

The claim must be notified within six (6) months and claim documents submitted within twelve (12) months of date of death.

Death Benefit — Documents Required
  • Original certified copy of Death certificate.
  • Completed death claim form obtainable from Bona Life.
  • KYC documents (proof of source of income, proof of residence, certified copy of ID/passport).
Total and Permanent Disability Benefit — Documents Required
  • Certificate of employer.
  • Valid medical report.
  • KYC documents (proof of source of income, proof of residence, certified copy of ID/passport).

Bona Life Insurance reserves the right to call for any additional documentation to ensure that the claim is valid. If any claim under this policy is fraudulent in any manner all benefits will be forfeited.

Total and Permanent Disability shall mean total and permanent inability, as a result of injury or sickness of a Life Assured to perform his/her own occupation or suited occupation by virtue of his/her own education, training, knowledge and experience, as determined by Babereki Life Insurance Company (Pty) Ltd. in its sole discretion.

The Death Benefit paid in respect of a Life Assured under this Policy shall be reduced by the amount of any Disability Benefit previously paid in respect of the Life Assured under this Policy.

The life insurance benefit will not be paid in the event of death being a direct result of:

  1. The Assured committing suicide in 12 months of his/her effective date in the event of death.
  2. Invasion, rebellion, war or war-like operation (whether war be declared or not), or from any involvement in any riot or civil commotion (whether participation was active or passive).
  3. Wilful self-inflicted injury, use of narcotics or abuse of alcohol.
  4. Extreme sports including but not limited to bungee jumping, diving, mountain biking, scuba diving, parasailing, paragliding, barefoot skiing, free flying, zip-lining, motorcycling and BMX racing, ski jumping, kayaking, surfing and shark cage diving.
  5. The effects of radioactivity or nuclear explosion.
  6. Involvement in criminal activity.
  7. Non-compliance to medical treatment.

No Disability Benefit shall be payable under this Policy during the first twelve (12) months following the Life Assured’s Entry Date if, in the opinion of the Insurer, the claim arises from any disease or condition which the Life Assured was diagnosed with, treated for, or displayed symptoms of within the six months prior to the Life Assured’s Entry Date.

The insurer reserves the right to amend, revoke, vary or alter any terms and conditions of this policy. However, the insured will be notified. This policy is renewable on an annual basis.
