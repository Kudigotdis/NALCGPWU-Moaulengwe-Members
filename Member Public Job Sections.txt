To ensure the user profile setup and staff admin dashboard capture precise operational, payroll, and organizational data, the system needs to categorize members across the four primary public employment sectors in Botswana.

NACL&PMWU / NALCGPWU represents industrial, operational, technical, and general support workers (historically known as the Manual Workers Union), alongside administrative and support staff.

1. Central Government (Ministries & Departments)
This covers line ministries, government departments, and national public works projects where salaries are processed directly via government payroll (DPSM/GABS).

Key Sub-Sectors / Departments
Ministry of Transport & Public Works: Buildings, roads, mechanical electrical services (DEMS), and central transport organization (CTO).

Ministry of Health: Government hospitals, primary clinics, and health post support staff.

Ministry of Agriculture: Agricultural research, veterinary services, field offices, and livestock stations.

Ministry of Education & Skills Development: Government schools, boarding facilities, and regional education offices.

Ministry of Environment & Tourism: National parks, wildlife conservation, forestry, and waste management teams.

Standard Job Titles & Cadres
Infrastructure & Trades: Roads Maintenance Worker, General Artisan (Plumber, Electrician, Carpenter, Welder), Painter, Mason, CTO Driver / Heavy Equipment Operator, Plant Operator.

Health & Sanitation: Health Auxiliary Worker, Hospital Orderly, Laundry Attendant, Clinical Kitchen Worker, Groundsman / Yard Assistant, Pest Control Officer.

Services & Support: Office Messenger, Cleaner, Storekeeper / Inventory Assistant, Security Guard, Night Watchman, Field Assistant (Agriculture/Fisheries).

2. Local Authorities (District & Urban Councils)
This encompasses City Councils (e.g., Gaborone, Francistown), Town Councils (e.g., Lobatse, Jwaneng), and District Councils (e.g., Kweneng, Central District).

Key Sub-Sectors / Departments
Civil Engineering & Infrastructure: Local road maintenance, storm water drainage, and public building upkeep.

Public Health & Environmental Services: Municipal waste collection, landfill sites, and public market management.

Social & Community Development: Community centers, primary school facilities management, and local social welfare support.

Commercial Services: Abattoirs, bus ranks, and public cemeteries.

Standard Job Titles & Cadres
Municipal Operations: Refuse Collector / Loader, Refuse Truck Driver, Street Cleaner, Landfill Attendant, Market Supervisor Assistant.

Community & Facility Maintenance: Abattoir Worker, Cemetery Attendant, Primary School Caretaker / Cook, Sewerage Works Operator.

Technical Support: Pipefitter, Maintenance Technician, Council Yard Watchman, Traffic Sign Installer.

3. Land Boards & Sub-Land Boards
This covers regional Land Boards (e.g., Malete Land Board, Tlokweng Land Board, Ngwato Land Board) and their respective Sub-Land Boards responsible for tribal land allocation and management.

Key Sub-Sectors / Departments
Land Administration & Allocation: Field surveying, plot demarcation, and land mapping support.

Compliance & Inspection: Land use monitoring and squatter control.

Administrative & Field Operations: Logistics, fleet management, and regional office administration.

Standard Job Titles & Cadres
Field Cadres: Land Board Field Assistant, Survey Assistant, Demarcation Assistant, Land Inspection Assistant.

Logistics & General Support: Land Board Driver (4x4 / All-Terrain Operators), Office Cleaner, Messenger, Yardman, Security Guard.

Administrative Cadres: Registration Clerk, Records Assistant, Customer Service Support Clerk.

4. Parastatals & State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs)
This sector covers semi-autonomous state-owned enterprises, public utilities, and regulatory bodies where union members are employed under parastatal bargaining agreements.

Primary Parastatal Entities Included
Utilities: Water Utilities Corporation (WUC), Botswana Power Corporation (BPC).

Transportation & Logistics: Botswana Railways (BR), Air Botswana (AB).

Agriculture & Housing: Botswana Meat Commission (BMC), Botswana Housing Corporation (BHC), Botswana Agricultural Marketing Board (BAMB).

Communications & Postal: Botswana Post (BotswanaPost), Botswana Telecommunications Corporation (BTC).

Standard Job Titles & Cadres
Utilities & Maintenance: Meter Reader, Water Pipefitter / Mains Repairer, Line Assistant (Electrical), Pump Station Operator, Plant Maintenance Worker.

Logistics & Warehousing: Warehouse Assistant, Grain Handler (BAMB), Postal Courier / Delivery Driver, Freight Handler (Botswana Railways).

Facilities & Support: Abattoir Processing Assistant (BMC), Estate Maintenance Helper (BHC), General Utility Assistant.

The key thing I recommend is: **do not implement this as only a two-tier Sector → Department flow.** For a robust NALCGPWU/Mokaulengwe platform, onboarding should progressively build a member's employment identity:


That structure will make the app far more useful later for **eligibility, payroll deductions, benefits, loans, claims, staff dashboards, analytics, and union representation**.

# 8. STEP 4 — Specific Job Title

After the user selects a cadre group, populate the final dropdown dynamically.

Example:

```javascript
function populateJobTitles() {

    const jobSelect =
        document.getElementById(
            "job-title"
        );

    jobSelect.innerHTML =
        `<option value="">
            Select your job
        </option>`;

    const jobs =
        getJobsForSelection(
            onboardingData.employment
        );

    jobs.forEach(job => {

        const option =
            document.createElement("option");

        option.value = job;

        option.textContent = job;

        jobSelect.appendChild(option);

    });

}
```

Always include:

```text
Other / Not Listed
```

When selected:

```text
Please enter your official job title.

[______________________]
```

This is important because union membership records may contain legacy, government-specific, or unusual job classifications.
